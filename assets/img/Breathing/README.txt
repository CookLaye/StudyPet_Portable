Breathing exercise pictures (put them in THIS folder)

Per pet (Pet = Cat, Dog, Axolotl, Raccoon or Penguin):
    <Pet>_Breathe_In.png     pet breathing in
    <Pet>_Hold.png           pet holding its breath
    <Pet>_Breathe_Out.png    pet breathing out

Example:  Dog_Breathe_In.png, Dog_Hold.png, Dog_Breathe_Out.png

Shared pictures for ALL pets (used when a pet has no pictures of its own):
    Breathe_In.png, Hold.png, Breathe_Out.png

PNG with a transparent background looks best. Any size is fine (it is scaled to fit).
If a picture is missing, the pet's normal picture is shown and it grows/shrinks with the breath.
The second "Hold" (lungs empty) reuses the Breathe_Out picture.
